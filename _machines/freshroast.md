* __Producer:__ [HOME ROASTING SUPPLIES](https://homeroastingsupplies.com/){:target="_blank"}, US
* __Machines:__ SR900
* __Connection:__ Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
* __Features:__ 
   - logging of bean temperature (BT) from the external probe and inlet temperature (as ET) from the internal sensor next to the heater, as well as heat and fan speed
   - heat and fan speed control
   - IO Command `sr900(<target>[,<value>])`
