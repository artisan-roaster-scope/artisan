* __Machines:__ All machines equipped with Unitronics PLC
* __Connection:__ Network via MODBUS TCP
* __Features:__
   - logging of bean temperature (BT) and exhaust temperature (XT), gas, airflow and drum speed
   - control of gas, airflow and drum speed
   - autoCHARGE and autoDROP support triggered by control signals from the machine
